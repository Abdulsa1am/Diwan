# QA sweep, 2026-10-27

Five Sonnet testers each covered one area of the local build of `b6ac5d6` (`npm run build` + `npm run verify` passed). They used headless Edge via Playwright at 320/375/412/768/1280px in the light, dark and navy themes. The lead reproduced the five most serious findings (marked ✔). No code or data was changed.

Areas: (1) shell, home, saved, about, 404; (2) poem reader; (3) `/diwan/` and `/search/`; (4) indexes and lexicon; (5) tools (atlas, wheel, relations, phrases, compare, hijaz).

**Overall:** no console errors, failed requests, horizontal overflow or duplicate h1 on any page, width or theme. All 685 poems render text identical to `data/edition/data.json` (no raw `#`, no zero-width or bidi characters). The testers crawled every internal link (1,405 on the indexes, every result/verse/place link) and each returned 200 or a valid `#vN`. `npm test` passes. The old URLs (`/explore/openings`, `/research/observatory`, `/relation/…`, `/poem/…/verse/N`) were checked on the live site and return the correct 301s.

| A3-01 ✔ | `/search/` | Words split across the halves (tatweel before `#`) can't be found: «بالخيف» misses r1-008 v4 (`بالخيـ # ف`). ## Low

- **Shell and home:**
  - **A1-01:** the settings panel stays open when focus Tabs out.
  - **A1-02:** the skip link leaves focus on `body`, because `#main` has no `tabindex="-1"`.
  - **A1-03:** «عن الشريف الرضي» has 167 links under 44px (section tabs are 40px).
  - **A1-07:** the 404 page offers only «افتح الديوان»; there's no search box or home link.
  - **A1-08:** the theme isn't synced across open tabs.
- **«محفوظاتي»:**
  - **A1-04:** the page shifts on load (CLS 0.083 at 320px).
  - **A1-05:** a tampered `radi-saved` (a very long title or a fake id) causes overflow or a 404 link.
  - **A1-06:** the undo bar never expires.
- **Poem reader:**
  - **A2-06:** the verse menu opens 450ms after a tap, even on the number itself.
  - **A2-07:** «حواشٍ أخرى» leaves focus on `body`.
  - **A2-08:** the reading bar shows a Latin total («من 119») until the first scroll (`Base.astro:123`; use `ar()`).
  - **A2-09:** at 320px and 132%, sizes within one poem range from 23 to 31px.
  - **A2-10:** small CLS (0.01–0.02) from the first fit.
  - **A2-11:** residual notes restart their numbering, and some end in «:» (a known text item).
- **Search and catalogue:**
  - **A3-04:** «كل الألفاظ» is AND within one verse, and the empty state suggests the mode that is already active.
  - **A3-05:** Arabic-Indic digits and symbol-only queries give no feedback.
  - **A3-06:** a very long query is echoed in full in the live region.
  - **A3-07:** search groups digits («١٤٬٧٥٩»); the catalogue doesn't.
  - **A3-08:** the live region announces three messages per search.
  - **A3-09:** without JS, dead disabled controls stay on screen.
  - **A3-10:** `/diwan/` adds `?page=1` to the URL. Add a canonical before indexing.
  - **A3-11:** «إزالة القيود» doesn't clear the text filter.
- **Indexes:**
  - **A4-03:** Back from a poem closes the open groups and scrolls to the top.
  - **A4-04:** links on «الإحصاءات» are 28px tall.
  - **A4-05:** lexicon and matali inputs are 38–40px, with no empty-state sentence.
  - **A4-06 (suspected):** 109 of 329 word pages list no other occurrences, possibly because the headword is matched with «ال».
  - **A4-07:** very long pages have no back-to-top control.
- **Tools:**
  - **A5-07:** the wheel's «سين» label is clipped, and 18 of 28 slices have no label. Counts appear only in `<title>`.
  - **A5-08:** relations has 12.7k DOM nodes (20.8k px tall at 375), 40px chips and some labels without hamza.
  - **A5-09:** the phrase tree matches only the exact surface form, and «ال» or «و» still build a tree.
  - **A5-10:** compare gives no notice when the same poem is picked twice; tools use `replaceState`, so Back doesn't step through selections; unlinked hijaz terms look like the linked chips.
  - **A5-11:** smooth-scrolling to a verse in a long poem takes up to 2.5s.

## Performance (measured)

- **Typical page:** 6–9 requests, 110–190KB transferred (fonts included), 6.7KB of JS on a poem page. On the longest poems at 4× CPU, p95 frame time is 16.9ms.
- **Search index:** 1.74MB raw, 547KB gzip, 433KB brotli. It loads once, on first focus. On slow 3G the first search waits a long time with only «جارٍ إعداد البحث…». After that, a search takes 0.3–0.5s (one long task of 180–260ms).
- **`/diwan/`:** 58.8KB gzipped and 4.5k DOM nodes. LCP is 588ms at 4× CPU, with no CLS.
